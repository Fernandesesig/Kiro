# Product

SIGrh (Sistema Integrado de Gestão de Recursos Humanos) is a Brazilian government HR management platform for public servants. It centralizes:

- Personnel records and registration (Recadastramento)
- Functional links/contracts (Vínculos) — effective, commissioned, temporary
- Payroll processing (Folha de Pagamento)
- Attendance and frequency control (Frequência)
- Leave and absences (Afastamentos)
- Staff movements and transfers (Movimentação)
- Career progression and promotions (Progressão, Promoção)
- Benefits and financial advantages (Vantagens Pecuniárias)
- Organizational structure management
- Selective processes (Processo Seletivo)

The system serves HR users, managers, and administrators within public sector agencies (Órgãos) grouped under administrative clusters (Agrupamentos). The UI language is Brazilian Portuguese — all labels, messages, and content must be in pt-BR.

## Key Domain Terms
- Vínculo: employment link/contract between a server and an agency
- Matrícula: employee registration number (format: `XXXXX-XX`)
- Servidor: public servant
- Órgão: government agency
- Agrupamento: administrative cluster grouping multiple agencies
- Carreira: career track (e.g., Controladoria Geral)
- Item de Carreira: specific position within a career (Grupo Ocupacional + Cargo)
- Regime de Trabalho: work regime (Estatutário, CLT, Administrativo)
- Regime de Previdência: social security regime (RPPS, RGPS)
